# Design and engineering references

Research guides the original implementation. Force values, difficulty timings, geometry and layouts are game design choices rather than values prescribed by documentation. Source, native behavior, inspected visuals, hardware acceptance and public distribution each require their own evidence.

## Connected bodies, movement and combat

[Unity ConfigurableJoint](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/class-ConfigurableJoint.html) documents locked linear axes, angular limits, drives and projection. Fifteen-region wrestlers retain permanent body joints with infinite break force/torque. Bounded root/torso forces and joint pose drives provide physical support and recovery instead of teleporting a skeleton into a standing pose. [Rigidbody.AddForce](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.AddForce.html) distinguishes force and impulse modes used for walking, shots, throws and contact.

[NVIDIA PhysX joint guidance](https://nvidiagameworks.github.io/PhysX/4.1/documentation/physxguide/Manual/Joints.html) explains projection behavior in cyclic constraint graphs. The game uses equal/opposite bounded spring grips at contact-local anchors instead of adding reciprocal native hand joints to the body graph. Stamina/strain affect hand grip release; anatomical joints remain attached.

[Unity Physics.IgnoreCollision](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.IgnoreCollision.html) supplies pair-specific exclusions. Only the fourteen directly connected body pairs are ignored because neighboring anatomical capsules overlap at joints. Nonadjacent body regions collide. Rounded torso/hip meshes and continuous collars are visual geometry; capsule contacts and permanent joints are separate physical mechanisms. Render inspection is needed to assess visible continuity and clipping.

The simulation uses a 1/240-second step with 64 position and 24 velocity solver iterations. Planted-foot targets, ground support, bounded movement forces, contact filtering and recovery protection address idle fumbling and movement under resistance. Q initiates a forward physical shot, while independent hand buttons acquire grips. X is a separate held-grip shove. Pull/pivot leverage, momentum throws, stamina brace and directional escape provide readable choices and openings. Current packaged tests and human playtesting must establish their actual outcomes.

[Unity joint and ragdoll stability](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/RagdollStability.html) recommends disabling joint preprocessing when collision geometry creates conflicting constraints. Anatomical joints retain projection and disable preprocessing. Fast turns release horizontal world-foot planting targets while preserving vertical support, preventing opposite boots from chasing crossing targets. Grip-spring stability also depends on contact-point effective mass, including the hand's rotational inertia; a force cap alone does not guarantee a stable explicit damping step. Current stress checks measure whole-body speed, energy trends, recovery and anatomical separation.

[PhysX mass scaling guidance](https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.4.0/docs/Joints.html#mass-scaling) states that altered joint mass/inertia can increase energy and fail to conserve momentum. All anatomical joint mass scales are one, so contact constraints use the actual region masses. The current implicit grip spring solves its contact response using translational mass and rotational inertia and keeps equal/opposite forces, existing grip strength and release behavior.

## Camera and AI

[Rigidbody interpolation](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody-interpolation.html) supports rendered body motion. [SmoothDamp](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Vector3.SmoothDamp.html) informs the stable follow focus, with different horizontal and vertical response. [SphereCast](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.SphereCast.html) supports obstruction probing; separate overlap checks handle starting/destination overlap limitations.

Exactly one camera follows the controlled wrestler. Obstruction distance, side choice and delayed return are filtered separately from body wobble. [Camera.ScreenPointToRay](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Camera.ScreenPointToRay.html) produces mouse aim rays through that camera's stable focus plane. Opponents do not drive framing or zoom. Native window captures include UI; camera render-texture captures establish scene rendering only.

[Physics.Raycast](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.Raycast.html) provides floor probes for AI steering alongside obstacle probes. AI difficulties change think cadence, reaction delay, openings and tactics, while using the same commands, stamina and force limits as humans. These probes are local steering aids rather than perfect navigation. Training switches passive/resisting/attacking behavior; a resisting partner only braces and steps while held, leaving periodic openings.

## Controls and private networking

[Unity serialization rules](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/script-serialization-rules.html) explain why a saved empty public ScriptedInputs array could override normal input and crash indexing. Test inputs remain NonSerialized, clear on startup and require an explicit automated run plus valid length. [Input.GetKey](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Input.GetKey.html) supplies held state; action edges are buffered between Update and FixedUpdate. The dedicated Keybinds page saves keyboard/mouse and controller action mappings and resets defaults.

[OnApplicationFocus](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/MonoBehaviour.OnApplicationFocus.html) informs automatic offline focus pause/resume. Utility keys cover F1 controls, Escape pause, F11 fullscreen and T Training reset. [Microsoft XInput](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/xinput/getting-started-with-xinput) defines the single local controller's state and vibration APIs. Actual input hardware and rebinding remain hands-on checks.

[Cloudflare Quick Tunnels](https://developers.cloudflare.com/tunnel/get-started/quick-tunnels/) create anonymous temporary trycloudflare.com addresses without a domain/account. Addresses expire with the host helper and have no uptime guarantee. [Cloudflare WebSocket support](https://developers.cloudflare.com/network/websockets/) permits a single long-lived upgraded connection; heartbeats prevent idle connections and host/client status handles disconnection. The official helper is pinned to [cloudflared 2026.10.0](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/releases/tag/2026.10.0), verified against the official release SHA256 and bundled with its license.

[Microsoft WebSocket.CreateFromStream](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.net.websockets.websocket.createfromstream) supplies framing after a bounded loopback HTTP upgrade. [ClientWebSocket](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.net.websockets.clientwebsocket) requires serialized sending and receiving. The bridge preserves existing bounded binary packets and HMAC challenge authentication. The room token travels in the invite's URL fragment and is removed from the WebSocket URL. [Unity .NET profiles](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/dotnet-profile-support.html) guide API compatibility; actual native Mono and public tunnel tests establish runtime behavior. The host must reach Cloudflare over its outbound tunnel connection; the friend uses ordinary HTTPS/WSS. See [tunnel network requirements](https://developers.cloudflare.com/tunnel/configuration/).

[Rigidbody.GetPointVelocity](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.GetPointVelocity.html) includes angular motion at contact, while [AddForceAtPosition](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody.AddForceAtPosition.html) applies force and torque through the contact. Momentum assistance is bounded, mass-aware and opposed by recoil/brace; comparative native trials are needed to establish spin and approach-speed advantage.

## GitHub updates and delivery

[GitHub Releases REST API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/releases/releases#get-the-latest-release) provides the public latest release and asset URLs for [the requested repository](https://github.com/zinovykargman-commits/wrestling). Source version is 0.2.0. A release manifest must agree with its tag, Windows archive URL, size and SHA256. Check and install are separate button presses; only a confirmed install downloads and restarts.

[Unity DownloadHandlerFile](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Networking.DownloadHandlerFile.html) writes the archive directly to disk. [Microsoft ZipFile](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.io.compression.zipfile) and [SHA256](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.security.cryptography.sha256) support extraction and integrity checks. Traversal, links, duplicates, unexpected package paths and expansion limits are checked. The bundled installer also verifies staged file length/hash against the verified archive, rejects extra/missing content, waits for game exit and retains backups on the installation drive for rollback. The helper is shipped locally rather than downloaded and executed as a script. Preferences are external and editable Source is preserved.

A correct local build does not prove publication or a successful update. Public asset hashes, native download checks, isolated installation/restart/rejection/rollback and current source/build manifests belong in VALIDATION.md. [Unity BuildPipeline.BuildPlayer](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/BuildPipeline.BuildPlayer.html) powers the editor build method. The three original ring-out arenas plus separate gym retain their distinct physics layouts; enjoyment, competitive balance and performance on another PC require human acceptance.
