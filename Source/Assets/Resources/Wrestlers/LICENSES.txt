# Separate character prototype provenance

The user authorized the realistic models in the Windows game. Exported body/garment meshes and textures are licensed for distribution under the terms below; the editable Blender master remains in this folder. Mac remains unchanged.

MakeHuman base topology, anatomical morphs, fitted hair (short04 and short01), eyebrow005, high-poly eyes and brown eye texture are from the MakeHuman community CC0 core/system asset collections. The system material files retain their original credit and license headers for Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Skin textures are from the CC0 Skins02 collection: Toigo young freckled male diffuse, Mindfront Aksel normal, and Mindfront African middle-age male diffuse/normal. Original author attribution and filenames remain with the editable assets.

- Core license: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html
- System pack: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- Skins pack: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html
- CC0 terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Fitted sports clothing, attached laces, lower-leg alignment, face fit, eye proportions, modest chest/biceps changes, materials, pose and studio setup are edited locally. No generative image stands in for an editable model. The Blender master is static; game exports use normalized official MakeHuman CC0 weights aggregated to the existing15 physical regions. Native current-version evidence is required for live attachment/deformation/network behavior. Official rig and weight files retain their CC0 metadata and copyright credits.
